# v50 Reader Font Weight Control

- Added reader body font-weight selector for 300 Light, 400 Regular, and 500 Medium. No bold weight is offered.
- Selection applies to Korean paragraphs and revealed original text, and is saved in local reader preferences and Google Drive user preferences.
- Existing preferences migrate automatically to weight 400 when no valid weight is stored.
- Google Fonts link already loads weights 300, 400, and 500 for Noto Sans KR, IBM Plex Sans KR, and Gothic A1.
- Versioned assets are `app-v50.css` and `app-v50.js`.
